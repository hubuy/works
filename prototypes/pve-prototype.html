Value rules
Create rules to tell us how much more certain audiences, placements, products and conversion locations are worth to your business. Our system will optimize for outcomes based on these rules. About value rules

How multiple rules work. When an audience, placement or product matches more than one rule, the highest bid adjustment is applied. Rules don't stack — so you can compose intent (e.g. "boost women's clothing +20%" alongside "boost FW26 hero products +50%") without worrying about runaway bid inflation.
